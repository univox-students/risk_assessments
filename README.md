# Recommended risk assessment entries and mitigation measure

The website is written to assist members of the University College, Oxford, to complete the risk assessments during the registration of high-risk events. The website documents possible risks and recommendations on how to mitigate them. The guide is inspired by the [guide from the Imperial College Union](https://imperialcollegeunion.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/101000556335-core-activity-risk-assessments).

The website is created by Samuel Lam (WCR External Affairs Officer 2023-26), and do not reflect the view of any societies he has involved in. Any mistakes made are almost surely personal.

> **How to use:** you can copy the hazard items and their suggested mitigation measures. Bear in mind the list is not exhaustive, and you should tailor-made your assessment for your events' needs.

Codes
🔴 - suitable for all events that involve this risk assessment entry
🟡 - suitable for some events that involve this risk assessment entry
C - College is responsible
O - Organiser is responsible

# 1. General

1. Crowd Management
    1. 🔴/O - appropriate site/sites chosen to accommodate given number of people
    1. 🔴/O - event attendees’ numbers to comply with fire safety policy
    1. 🔴/O - appropriate fire marshalling, with all marshals familiar with all emergency exits and have access to the accurate number of people in a venue (e.g., a synchronised counter)
    1. 🔴/O - emergency exits assessed and nothing blocking them ensured
    1. 🔴/O - emergency plan in place prior to event
    1. 🟡/O - preparation of attendees’ list
1. Slips, trips or falls
    1. 🔴/O - clear the floor of any potential trip hazards and check surfaces are level and free of damage
    1. 🔴/O - all tripping hazard to be clearly marked with appropriate signage
    1. 🟡/O - monitor for spillages and clean immediately.
    1. 🟡/O - appropriate cable management, e.g. performing area not accessible to general public, trailing cables covered / taped to the sides of the room
    1. 🟡/O - instruct attendees to put their belongings out of any walkways / leave the belongings out of the venues. 
1. Noise
    1. 🔴/O - events ending on time in accordance with the event policy
    1. 🔴/O - noise level outside of the venue monitored.
1. Electrical and fire safety
    1. 🔴/O - no open flame at site
    1. 🔴/O - use equipment that has been PAT/EET tested with the College (labelled with a valid PAT/EET testing sticker)
    1. 🔴/O - keep liquids away from electrical appliances
    1. 🔴/O - ensure good ventilation for the appliances, e.g., not being covered up
    1. 🔴/O - ensure sockets are not overloaded
    1. 🟡/O - all external equipment being PAT/EET tested with certificate sent to the hospitality prior to the events
1. Alcohol consumption
    1. 🔴/O - event not [irresponsibly promoted](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2440/schedules/made)
    1. 🔴/CO - quantities to adhere to the recommended units per person (two drinks per person)
    1. 🔴/CO - drinks are available to customers in the [required measures](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2440/schedules/made), with options clearly displayed in a menu
    1. 🔴/CO - non-alcoholic options are available
    1. 🔴/CO - ensure easy access to free and portable drinking water
    1. 🔴/CO - ensure assistance when attendees are feeling unwell, e.g., [organisers aware of angel shots](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/blog/4096/what-is-an-angel-shot.html?srsltid=AU7gw4XYwNLKwD2qeZ5TtkvgupWkE1OROOUgz10c8Chw9-ru2b9A4K5X)
    1. 🟡/O - alcohol sourced *solely* from the College
    1. 🟡/O - alcohol sourced from a reliable source (e.g. supermarkets), with copies of receipts properly documented
1. Manual handling (transporting heavy objects)
    1. 🔴/O - sufficient helpers acquired
    1. 🔴/O - helpers are familiar with the correct lifting postures (bending the knees and legs rather than the back, and avoid torso twisting)
    1. 🔴/O - heavy item broken up into lighter loads whenever possible
    1. 🟡/O - usage of mechanical aids 
1. Cash handling (Cashbox)
    1. 🔴/O - the person handling the cash is trustworthy
    1. 🔴/O - the receipts for both organisers and customers could be provided (e.g., receipt book)
    1. 🔴/O - cash to be deposited back to the organisers' bank account as soon as possible, and within 7 days after the event
1. Conflict with 3rd parties suppliers
    1. 🔴/O - contracts carefully checked before confirming, noting payment and return arrangements
    1. 🔴/O - correspondences, invoices and receipts properly documented
    1. 🔴/O - if they need access to the College (e.g., to set up), they should be ushered by at least a member of the organiser, whom have easy access to College assistance (porters or junior deans)
1. Loss or damaged property
    1. 🔴/O - well-documented inventory
    1. 🟡/C - College monitoring items stored within the College premises
    1. 🟡/C - items properly tracked by technology, e.g. AirTag
1. Theft
    1. 🔴/CO - clear signage encouraging attendees to look after the belongings, and that the College and organisers are not responsible for the loss of personal belongings
    1. 🔴/CO - appropriate monitoring of the events by the organisers and the College

# 2. Welfare of attendees
1. Attendees conduct
    1. 🔴/O - organisers are aware of steps to [intervene as an active bystander](https://www.activebystander.co.uk/how-to-intervene/) should there be a conflict or misconduct
    1. 🔴/O - organisers have easy access to College assistance (porters or junior deans)
1. Sensitive/triggering contents
    1. 🟡/O - appropriate content notice published in event advertisements for known triggering contents
1. External attendance
    1. 🔴/O - meeting points confirmed for external attendees
    1. 🔴/O - attendees are attended by at least one College member
    1. 🔴/O - all external attendees are aware of the [College's expectation on their conducts](https://www.univ.ox.ac.uk/policy-documents/) (this could come as a form of informal reminder)
    1. 🟡/CO - directions to venue, toilets and exits clearly signposted
1. Minors (Under 18)
    1. 🔴/O - sufficient safeguarding if alcohol is involved, e.g., bartenders being able to identify minor attendees and not providing them with alcohols
    1. 🟡/C - sufficient safeguarding for film rating 18+, e.g., rating displayed, minor attendees not allowed for films with adult rating
    1. 🟡/C - for events that involve specialist trainings (see 3) or physical activities (see 4), there are evidence of parental consent
1. Accessibility
    1. 🔴/CO - accessibility plan in place prior to the event, e.g., organisers knowing who to contact for access to lift
    1. 🟡/CO - accessible routes are cleared and signposted
1. Other welfare concerns

# 3. Self or external catering

1. Allergies and cross-contamination
    1. 🔴/O - food sourced from a reliable source (e.g. supermarkets), with copies of receipts properly documented
    1. 🔴/O - sufficient options provided for people with allergies or preferences, e.g. vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, lactose intolerance...
    1. 🔴/O - reasonable labelings for the food provided
1. Food poisoning
    1. 🔴/O - if live preparation of food is required, there should be a supervisor that has completed an appropriate level of food safety training (e.g. Level 2 Food Safety and Hygiene for Catering)
    1. 🔴/O - access to assistance readily available (e.g. porters, junior deans)
    1. 🟡/O - organisers are first-aid trained
1. Hot liquids
    1. 🔴/O - ensure helpers are aware of the safety handling of hot liquid
    1. 🟡/C - reasonably clear signage (e.g. if it is a container of coffee, then it should be clearly indicated)

# 5. For events that involve physical activities
1. Exhaustion
     1. 🔴/O - planned breaks
     1. 🔴/O - attendees have the freedom to pause/stop the activity in a safely manner
1. Personal injury
     1. 🔴/O - access to assistance readily available (e.g. porters, junior deans)
     1. 🟡/O - organisers are first-aid trained
1. Physical contact
1. Poor / damaged equipment
     1. 🔴/O - well-documented inventory with maintenance logs
1. Inexperienced participants
     1. 🔴/O - clear statement on the suitability of inexperienced participants
     1. 🔴/O - safety plans in place for inexperienced participants
     1. 🔴/O - welfare provision in place if the participant feels discouraged
